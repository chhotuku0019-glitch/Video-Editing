# Subtitles, Typography, Color
## Subtitles / on-screen text
- Not continuous. Only key words, key phrases, facts, names, numbers, essential dialogue.
- Skip where visuals already carry the meaning.
- Max one primary text element plus one supporting line at a time. Never crowd the frame.
- Safe margins: long-form 5% sides, 8% bottom; Shorts keep clear of the bottom ~20% and right ~12% (platform UI) and top ~10%.
- Hindi/Hinglish: use a font that renders Devanagari properly (e.g. Noto Sans Devanagari, Hind, Mukta, Poppins for Latin). Test-render a frame and inspect that conjuncts/matras are correct before using.
## Typography as design
Hierarchy (heading vs support), contrast of weights (bold vs light), one hero word enlarged, tight alignment grid, balanced spacing, subtle shadow/shape behind text when needed, animation synced to scene motion (ease in/out, no bounce spam). One or two font families per video. Font suits subject and references. Check license: prefer open fonts (OFL).
## Color and grading
Choose one palette per video from topic + references (e.g. navy/cyan/white/grey, warm sand/charcoal, etc.). Write it in editing_notes. Consistent across scenes. No gratuitous bright red/green/yellow backgrounds; accents only with purpose. Text/background contrast at least ~4.5:1. Natural cinematic grade, restrained saturation, consistent skin tones and black levels across clips.
